\# SCMS — Usability Check



\## Purpose



This usability check was conducted to evaluate the proposed Student Complaint Management System (SCMS) user flows and dashboard interfaces. The test focused on whether students could submit and track complaints and whether administrators could find, filter, and review complaints.



The test also aimed to identify areas where the interface or terminology could be improved.



\---



\## Participants



Five participants were involved:



\- 3 student participants

\- 2 administrator-role participants



The participants were asked to complete representative tasks using the proposed SCMS interface screens.



\---



\## Test Method



The usability check was conducted as a moderated task-based evaluation.



Participants were told:



> "I'm testing this design, not you. There are no right or wrong answers. I want to see how easy or difficult the design is to understand."



Participants were asked to think aloud while completing the tasks. The facilitator recorded task completion, approximate time, observations, and participant comments.



The student evaluation used the Student Dashboard and Submit New Complaint screens.



The administrator evaluation used the Administrator Dashboard/Review Queue screen.



\---



\# Student Usability Test



\## Student Tasks



\### Task 1 — Submit a Complaint



Participants were asked:



> "Imagine you are a student who has experienced a problem at the university. Show me how you would submit a complaint using this design."



\### Task 2 — Complete the Complaint Form



Participants were asked to complete a complaint involving a university problem and identify the required information and optional supporting documents.



\### Task 3 — Track a Complaint



Participants were asked:



> "You submitted a complaint two days ago. Show me how you would check what is happening with your complaint."



\### Task 4 — Understand Status



Participants were asked to explain the meaning of the complaint statuses:



\- Pending

\- Under Review

\- Resolved



\### Task 5 — Find an Update



Participants were asked:



> "The university has sent an update about your complaint. Where would you expect to find that update?"



\---



\# Student Results



\## Student 1



\### Task 1 — Submit Complaint



\- Result: Completed successfully

\- Time: 1 minute 10 seconds

\- Observation: Quickly identified the "Submit Complaint" option from the Student Dashboard and selected it without hesitation.

\- Comment: "This one is straightforward."



\### Task 2 — Complete Complaint Form



\- Result: Completed successfully

\- Time: 2 minutes 15 seconds

\- Observation: Understood Category, Subject, Detailed Description and Date of Incident. Recognized that supporting documents were optional and selected "Proceed to Review."

\- Comment: The form was "easy to understand" and the optional document section was clear.



\### Task 3 — Track Complaint



\- Result: Completed successfully

\- Time: 45 seconds

\- Observation: Selected "My Complaints", selected a recent complaint and used the displayed status to determine progress.

\- Comment: "I would check here to see if they have worked on it."



\### Task 4 — Understand Status



The participant understood all three statuses:



\- Pending: Nobody has started working on it yet.

\- Under Review: Someone is currently looking at it.

\- Resolved: The problem has been dealt with.



\### Task 5 — Find Update



\- Result: Completed successfully

\- Observation: Opened the complaint and expected the update to appear there.

\- Comment: "I'd expect updates to be inside the complaint."

\# Key Usability Findings



\## 1. Submission path is generally clear



Two of the three students quickly identified the Submit Complaint option. One student initially confused submitting a new complaint with viewing existing complaints.



\### Finding



The submission path is generally understandable, but the distinction between creating a new complaint and viewing existing complaints should be strengthened.



\---



\## 2. Category field needs additional guidance



One student was unsure what information should be entered under Category.



\### Finding



The Category field should provide a short explanation or examples to reduce uncertainty.



\---



\## 3. My Complaints and History can be confused



One student initially selected History when attempting to track a complaint.



\### Finding



The design should make My Complaints the clearly identifiable location for tracking active complaints, while History should communicate that it contains past activity.



\---



\## 4. Under Review needs clearer explanation



Two students understood the status, while one student was uncertain about its meaning.



\### Finding



A short status explanation can make the meaning of Under Review clearer.



\---



\## 5. Complaint updates should be clearly located



All students eventually expected updates to appear inside the individual complaint, but one student was unsure whether to look in History, Notifications or the complaint.



\### Finding



The complaint details/tracking view should include a clearly labelled Updates or Communication section.



\---



\## 6. Administrator filtering works well for status and priority



Both administrators successfully used the filtering controls to find Pending and High Priority complaints.



\### Finding



Status and Priority filtering are understandable and should be retained.



\---



\## 7. Date filtering needs to be clearly visible



Both administrators identified difficulty finding complaints for a particular date. One participant could not complete the task directly.



\### Finding



The Review Queue should provide a clearly visible Date filter alongside the existing Category, Priority and Status filtering controls.



\---



\# Design Revisions



Based on the usability findings, the following revisions are recommended:



\### Student experience



1\. Clearly distinguish "Submit New Complaint" from "My Complaints".

2\. Add helper text/examples to the Category field.

3\. Make My Complaints the primary location for tracking active complaints.

4\. Clarify the purpose of History as past activity.

5\. Add a short explanation for the Under Review status.

6\. Add a clearly labelled Updates/Communication section within complaint details.



\### Administrator experience



1\. Retain the existing Status and Priority filters.

2\. Ensure the Date filter is clearly visible in the Review Queue.

3\. Keep the Actions/View mechanism for opening complaint details.



\---



\# Revision Summary



| Area | Finding | Revision |

|---|---|---|

| Student navigation | Submit Complaint and My Complaints were confused by one participant | Make New Complaint and My Complaints visually distinct |

| Category | One participant was unsure what Category meant | Add helper text/examples |

| Tracking | History was mistaken for complaint tracking | Make My Complaints the clear tracking location |

| Status | Under Review was slightly unclear to one participant | Add explanatory status text |

| Updates | One participant was unsure where updates would appear | Add Updates/Communication section to complaint details |

| Admin filtering | Status and Priority filters worked well | Retain current approach |

| Admin date search | Both participants looked for a date filter | Make Date filter clearly visible |



\---



\# Limitations



This usability check involved a small sample of five participants: three students and two administrator-role participants.



The evaluation used static interface screens rather than a fully functional SCMS application. Therefore, the test focused on navigation expectations, information architecture, terminology, and visual discoverability rather than actual system functionality.



The findings should therefore be treated as an initial usability check rather than a statistically representative evaluation of all SCMS users.



\---



\# Conclusion



The usability check showed that the main student and administrator workflows were generally understandable.



Students were generally able to identify the complaint submission and tracking paths, while administrators were able to use status, priority and complaint actions effectively.



The main improvements identified were clearer distinction between My Complaints and History, additional guidance for the Category field, clearer explanation of the Under Review status, a dedicated location for complaint updates, and a clearly visible Date filter for administrators.



These findings were used to guide revisions to the SCMS UI/UX flows and dashboard designs.
